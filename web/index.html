Findings.

Computed from the data above, not written by hand.

    Method. Every rating counted here is a star rating that came with a written comment. Themes are tagged by keyword (English and Arabic) and the same tagging is used for every app. One and two stars are negative, four and five positive, three resolved by wording. ADCB combines two app generations: the new ADCB. app (April 2026 onward) and the previous ADCB app.

    Sources: Apple App Store reviews via the public RSS feed and the apps.apple.com catalogue API; Google Play reviews via the store’s review endpoint.
